Our Story

About SoulLink Co.

Founded in 2020, Soul'Link Co. is India's emerging quantitative hedge fund built on the premise that technology, data, and disciplined algorithmic trading can consistently outperform traditional investment approaches.

Our team of data scientists, financial engineers, and ML researchers develops proprietary trading systems operating across Indian indices, forex, commodities, and digital assets.

Since inception, we have maintained a target payout of 8% p.m* for our investors, backed by rigorous risk controls and full operational transparency.
