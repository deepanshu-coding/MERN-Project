Privacy Policy

Last Updated · January 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

SoulLink Co. collects personal information including your name, email address, mobile number, Aadhaar number, PAN card number, and bank account details when you register or invest. We also collect transaction data, usage data, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and comply with KYC/AML regulations
  • To process investments, monthly payouts, and withdrawals
  • To communicate important account and fund updates
  • To improve our products, services, and algorithms
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard AES-256 encryption for all sensitive data at rest and in transit. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plaintext. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

4. Aadhaar & PAN Data

Aadhaar and PAN data is collected solely for KYC verification. We do not store raw Aadhaar numbers beyond the verification process. Data is handled in compliance with UIDAI guidelines and India's data protection framework.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. Data may be shared with regulatory authorities, payment processors, or as required by Indian law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact: soullinkco.pvt.ltd@gmail.com

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential session storage for authentication and page state. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
